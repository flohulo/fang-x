# Bonus mission for X

**Setting in Fang X:** Enable bonus mission for X
**Rule parameter:** `objective.enabled`
**Default value:** Off

With the bonus mission, X can pick a secret intermediate goal in the play area during the round.
If X gets within 50 meters of the goal, the next location report is postponed once by two
minutes.

## How it works

1. X taps **Choose target on map** in the app and sets the point on the map.
2. The target has to be inside the play area and far enough from the current location.
3. If X gets within 50 meters of the point, the next reveal is postponed once by two minutes.

## Tips for a safe round

- Choose an achievable target in a public place.
- Do not choose private, closed-off or dangerous places.
- The mission is optional and complements the normal escape and catch rules.
- The bonus mission does not prevent the live location share if X leaves the play area.
