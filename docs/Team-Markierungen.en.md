# Team markers

Detectives can mark places on the map and coordinate without describing coordinates in the chat.
Only detectives see the markers, **X never sees them**.

## How to use them

1. Tap **Mark suspect** or **Mark not here** at the bottom of the map controls.
2. Tap the map where you want to place the marker.
3. The marker appears for all detectives immediately.

| Marker | Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Suspect | orange with **?** | X might be here |
| Not here | grey with **×** | We checked here, X is not here |

Tap a marker to see who placed it and when. You can also **remove** it there. Any detective may do
this, so outdated hints do not stay on the map.

## Rules

- Marking works during the head start and the running round, for detectives only.
- There are at most 20 markers at a time. When you place another one, the oldest one drops off.
- The host and X cannot place markers.

## Privacy

Markers are freely chosen points, not player locations. They are only kept in memory, are not
stored, and are cleared at the start and end of the round.
