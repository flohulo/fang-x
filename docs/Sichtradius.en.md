# Reveal radius

**Setting in Fang X:** Reveal radius (meters)
**Rule parameter:** `reveal.revealRadiusMeters`
**Default value:** 5 meters

The reveal radius determines how precisely the detectives see a regular reveal of X on the map.
With a larger radius the circle shows a search area instead of an almost exact point.

## Which value should I choose?

- **5 meters** show the reveal very precisely.
- **50–100 meters** leave more room for hiding places, decoys and searching the terrain.
- In densely built-up or confusing areas a larger radius can make the round more varied.

The reveal radius applies to regular reveals. With a live transmission after leaving the play
area, the exact location is shared. The frequency of reveals is controlled by the
[reveal interval](Reveal-Intervall.md).
