# Radio discipline and radio language

The [voice radio](Sprechfunk.md) is open to all detectives at the same time. Only one person
speaks at a time, everyone else listens. A few simple rules keep the chase from turning into
chaos: **keep it short, speak clearly, let others finish.**

This page is a suggestion for how to talk to each other. The app does not enforce any of it.

## The most important rules

1. **Listen first, then speak.** If the app shows that someone is speaking, wait until they are
   done. Two people at once cannot be understood.
2. **Think first, then press.** Work out your message before you hold the button.
3. **Keep it short.** A message is one or two sentences. Break longer instructions into parts and
   leave room for questions in between.
4. **Press, pause briefly, then speak.** Hold the button until the end of your sentence, not less,
   or the start or end gets cut off.
5. **Calm and clear.** Use a normal volume, do not speak faster than usual and keep the microphone
   close. Whispering or shouting is harder to understand.
6. **Game talk only.** The radio is for positions, the situation and coordination.
7. **Nothing personal.** Do not name full names, addresses or recognizable passers-by. Other
   people's privacy applies here too.

## Structure of a message

| Step | Content | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Who? | Your name or call sign | "Mia" |
| 2. To whom? | The group or one person | "to all" |
| 3. What? | The key point | "X spotted" |
| 4. Where? | Place, ideally with direction | "Station Street, heading north" |
| 5. How? | Movement, speed, detail | "walking, blue jacket" |
| 6. Closing | What happens next? | "Over" |

In practice: **"Mia to all. X spotted, Station Street, heading north, walking. Over."**

## Radio language

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Over** | I am done, someone may answer now. |
| **Out** | My message is complete, no answer needed. |
| **Understood** | I heard you and understood. |
| **Confirmed** | Yes, that is correct. |
| **Negative** | No, that is not correct. |
| **Say again** | I did not understand you. |
| **I repeat** | I will say it once more. |
| **Stand by** | I need a moment, please do not answer. |
| **Correction** | I misspoke, the following applies. |
| **All stations** | Message to all detectives. |

!!! tip "Not both"
    "Over" and "Out" exclude each other. "Over" expects an answer, "Out" does not.

Confirm important messages with a short **"Understood"**. For places and numbers, repeat the key
point: "Station Street north, understood."

Agree on short, distinct call signs before the start, for example colors ("Red", "Blue"). Avoid
names that sound alike.

## Giving locations

- Use **streets or squares** instead of "over there", or a clear landmark: "by the supermarket",
  "at the bridge".
- Use **compass directions** or directions relative to a place: "away from the station". Left and
  right are unclear because everyone is facing a different way.
- Give **time** in minutes when it matters: "two minutes ago at the traffic light".
- Be honest about **certainty**: "probably" is better than false confidence.
- If a place can be shown on the map, set a [team marker](Team-Markierungen.md) and say: "Marker
  set."

## Messages in the game

| Situation | Example |
|---|---|
| New reveal ([reveal interval](Reveal-Intervall.md)) | "Red to all. New reveal, market square, circle to the south. Over." |
| X spotted | "Blue to all. X spotted, Station Street, heading north, walking. Over." |
| Not sure | "Green to all. Possible sighting, park entrance, not sure. Over." |
| Nothing found | "Red to all. Old town empty, moving east. Out." |
| Changing position | "Blue to Red. I take the bridge, you take the square. Over." |
| Catch | "Green to all. X in catch radius, catching now. Over." |
| Question | "Red to Blue. Say again, did not understand the place. Over." |

## When things get difficult

- **Poor reception or noise.** Speak more slowly and keep it short. If that does not help, write
  important messages in the chat. It is visible to everyone, including X.
- **Nobody answers.** Ask once: "Blue, do you copy? Over." If nothing comes, say your message
  once in full anyway. Others may still hear it.
- **Two people speak at once.** Both stop. The person with the more important message starts
  again after a moment, the other waits.
- **The microphone does not work.** See the permissions section of the
  [voice radio](Sprechfunk.md) page.

## Emergencies

!!! danger "Real emergencies are not part of the game"
    For an injury, danger or any real emergency, call the emergency number (**112** in Europe). The voice radio does not replace it. The
    [emergency button](Notfall-Knopf.md) ends the round and shares your location if someone cannot
    continue.

Report a real emergency clearly and without game language: **"Emergency, emergency, emergency.
This is Mia, I am injured, Park Avenue."** Everyone then stops normal radio traffic and helps.

## Radio silence has two meanings

In radio traffic, **radio silence** means: nobody transmits. In the app there is also the
[radio silence warning](Alle-sicher-und-Funkstille.md). It has nothing to do with the voice radio:
it reports that no position has arrived from a person for a while.

## Quick checklist before the start

- [ ] All detectives have allowed the microphone and have briefly tested it.
- [ ] Call signs are agreed.
- [ ] Everyone knows "Over", "Out", "Understood" and "Say again".
- [ ] Headphones are charged and connected, see [voice radio](Sprechfunk.md).
- [ ] Everyone knows where the emergency button is and what the emergency number is.
